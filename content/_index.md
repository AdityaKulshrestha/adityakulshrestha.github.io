I'm a performance and solutions engineer at Intel, working on making AI models run fast and efficiently on real hardware, from GPUs and CPUs to AI PCs. My interests sit between machine learning systems and the silicon underneath them. I care about hardware-software codesign, where model design, software stacks and hardware capabilities are shaped together, with a particular focus on LLM inference, speech models and voice agents.

Before Intel I worked on voice and LLM products at startups, and did research on vision-language models at IIIT Delhi. I studied computer science at the Institute of Engineering and Technology, Lucknow.
